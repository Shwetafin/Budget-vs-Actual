# 📊 Budget vs Actual — Q1 FY 2026-27

> Variance analysis and management commentary comparing budgeted vs actual financial performance for Q1 FY 2026-27 for a SaaS company.

---

## 📌 Overview

This project presents a structured Budget vs Actual (BvA) variance analysis for Q1 FY 2026-27, examining financial performance across key cost and revenue heads using conditional formatting and a Waterfall chart. It quantifies variances (absolute and percentage) and provides management commentary to give finance teams and stakeholders a clear view of how the business performed relative to plan.

---

## ❗ Objective

To answer the question: "Where did we spend more or less than planned in Q1 FY 2026-27, and why?" built a clean, Excel model that highlights key areas of over and under-performance, so finance and business teams can pinpoint gaps, understand root causes, and course-correct early in the financial year.

## 🔍 Methods

1. **Variance Calculation** — Computed absolute variance (`Actual − Budget`) and percentage variance (`Variance / Budget × 100`) for each line item.
2. **Conditional Formatting** — Applied conditional formatting (green/red) for quick visual scanning, with ‘green’ being favourable and ‘red’ being unfavourable for each of the cost & Revenue line items.
3. **Summary Dashboard** — Used waterfall Chart of the variance bridge for executive review.
4. **Variance Commentary** — Written commentary added for key variances explaining the drivers, key risks and focus areas for Q2

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- 📈 **Revenue** — Actual revenue was **3.8% above** budget, driven by **Subscription, New MRR & Expansion MRR**.
- 💸 **Operating Expenses** — Total opex came was **1% over** budget, primarily due to **Sales Team Compensation and General & Administrative expenses**.
- ⚠️ **Largest Unfavourable Variance** — **Sales Team Compensation** exceeded budget by **$25K**, attributed to ** increase in commissions on performance, however, the cost is positively correlated with revenue outperformance.**.
- ✅ **Largest Favourable Variance** — **Subscription Revenue** which exceeded budget by **$1.1M**, **reflecting strong retention and expansion within the existing customer base **.

## ✅ Results & Conclusion

Q1 FY2026 represents a strong start to the financial year. Revenue and margin performance exceeded plan, and the business demonstrated solid operating leverage with EBITDA margin expanding meaningfully. Management focus for Q2 should centre on arresting churn, managing infrastructure unit economics, and clearing the headcount pipeline to sustain this trajectory through the remainder of FY2026
